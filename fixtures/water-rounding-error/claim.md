# AI water use is a rounding error

Example. A common sentiment about AI and water, written out as one
post. Assertions like these merit a more careful check.

---

The panic about AI and water is innumerate. Lawrence Berkeley
National Laboratory's 2024 data center report puts on-site water
consumption by all U.S. data centers at about 66 billion liters in
2023, roughly 17 billion gallons. The U.S. Geological Survey
estimates that crop irrigation in the lower 48 states withdrew
about 111 billion gallons a day in 2020 and consumed most of it.
Data centers come to a small fraction of one percent of that.
Nationally, it is a rounding error.

It is not even really used. The water evaporates and comes back as
rain, so nothing is lost.

So nobody who lives near a data center has anything to worry about.
If the national number is a rounding error, the local one is too.

And look at what comes out of those buildings: search, maps, email,
payments, hospital records. People use them every day.

The numbers are in. This one is settled, and it will stay settled.
