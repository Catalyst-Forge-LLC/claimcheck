# STOP blaming your shower!!!

Example. Written for ClaimCheck to calibrate tone versus literacy.

---

Honestly, it is EXHAUSTING watching people guilt-trip each other over
five-minute showers. Look at the actual numbers, people!

In 2015, per the USGS national water-use report (Circular 1441),
public supply — the water utilities deliver to homes and businesses —
was 39.0 billion gallons a day of withdrawals. Irrigation was 118
billion. Thermoelectric power withdrew 133 billion a day, but most of
that goes right back to the river: only 4.31 billion a day was
actually consumed. Irrigation consumed 73.2 billion a day.

So if you care about water that is actually used up, farms are the
story, not your bathroom. Those are national totals for 2015; your
own watershed may look very different, so check your local utility.
