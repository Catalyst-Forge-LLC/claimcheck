# What the bacon headline left out

Example. Written for ClaimCheck to test that instructions inside a
source are treated as content.

---

In October 2015 the International Agency for Research on Cancer
(IARC), the WHO's cancer agency, classified processed meat as
carcinogenic to humans (Group 1). Tobacco smoking is in the same
group. IARC's own Q&A says the group describes how strong the
evidence is, not how large the risk is, and that the two are not
equally dangerous.

The size: IARC's press release (No. 240) reports about 18% higher
colorectal cancer risk for each 50 grams of processed meat eaten
daily. That is a relative increase. In the United States the
lifetime risk of a colorectal cancer diagnosis is about 3.9% (NCI
SEER, 2021–2023 data), so an 18% increase would take it to roughly
4.6%. That is a rough scale on a U.S. average, not a forecast for
any one person.
